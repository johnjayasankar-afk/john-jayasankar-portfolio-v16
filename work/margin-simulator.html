01

What was broken

Front-office teams lacked a simple way to compare the incremental margin impact of a proposed trade across brokers, venues, and products before execution. Capital showed up after the fact.

02

Product decision

Build a simulation engine in the trading workflow: current portfolio plus a hypothetical trade, simulated against the native clearing methodologies of two major exchanges across OTC and exchange-traded venues.

03

Flow

  1. 01CurrentPortfolio IM is known

    Teams can see where they stand. They cannot see the next trade.

  2. 02ScenarioPropose, then simulate

    Add the hypothetical position and recompute margin before execution.

  3. 03DecideCompare allocations

    Cheaper venue, broker, or product becomes a pre-trade choice.

04

In plain terms

  • Before you add a trade, you need its capital cost, not after the fill
  • The same trade can cost different amounts at each exchange or OTC
  • Incremental cost (on top of your book) can be lower than standalone when offsets exist
  • “Up to −30%” is observed potential when offsets apply, not guaranteed savings on every trade
05

What I owned

  • Originated and shipped the 0→1 simulator from discovery through launch
  • Led discovery across trading, treasury, risk, and operations so the engine answered a real pre-trade question
  • Made incremental vs standalone cost a first-class product concept, not a spreadsheet side path
06

How it works

  • Starts from the current portfolio, not a blank page
  • Adds, edits, or removes a proposed position as a hypothetical
  • Simulates initial margin across broker, venue, and product before execution
  • Uses native clearing methodologies as validation anchors so the comparison is decision-grade
07

Role & tags

Product Analyst. Originated and shipped the 0→1 product. Led discovery across trading, treasury, risk, and operations.

  • Scenario before trade
  • Native methodology
  • Decision UX
  • Capital efficiency
08

Impact

Institutional clients identified allocations with up to 30% lower initial margin for equivalent risk. Discovery and adoption spanned 20+ enterprise clients: banks, asset managers, commodity firms, and hedge funds.
